How it works

Four steps. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

  1. Record a voice note

    Record your voice directly in the app, just like WhatsApp, or choose an existing audio file from your phone. Your message is sent as a WhatsApp voice message (Opus), not as a file attachment.

  2. Pick who gets it

    Choose someone from your phone contacts, or save a profile for people you message often. Works with WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business.

  3. Choose a time

    Pick any future date and time. Tap Schedule Message, and your voice note will wait until it's time to send.

  4. Approve when it rings

    At that time an alarm sounds and the approval screen opens, even on the lock screen. Tap Approve & Send. If the phone is locked, it asks for your PIN or fingerprint first. A note you don't approve within an hour is marked missed.

Good to know

Each note is sent once

After it goes out it's marked Sent and can never fire again.

You stay in control

Nothing leaves your phone without your tap on the approval screen.

What it asks for, and why

The app needs more access than most. Here is each permission in plain terms.

Accessibility serviceTaps the Send button inside WhatsApp at your scheduled time. Only active while a scheduled voice note is being sent.
Display over other apps, full-screen notificationsShows the approval screen when the phone is locked.
NotificationsTells you when a note is ready, sent, or missed.
Battery: unrestrictedKeeps the alarm on time instead of being delayed by battery saving.

Wake lock, vibrate, foreground serviceWakes the screen and rings the alarm, and keeps the send running until it finishes.
MicrophoneLets the app record audio.

Install it

Installed straight from the file, not from the Play Store.

  1. Download the APK with the button at the top of this page.
  2. Open the file. When Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps.
  3. Open Voice Scheduler and grant the permissions above: notifications, accessibility, display over other apps, and unrestricted battery.
  4. Schedule a test note two minutes from now.

Questions

Does it send messages without me?

No. At the scheduled time you get an approval screen and have to tap Approve & Send. If you don't, the note is marked missed after an hour.

Why does it need an accessibility service?

WhatsApp doesn't let other apps send a message on a schedule. Voice Scheduler opens the chat with your audio and taps Send for you. The service is only active while a scheduled note is going out.

Does the app connect to the internet?

The app doesn't request internet permission. Your audio is handed to WhatsApp on your phone, and the app itself uploads nothing.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. It works with both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. Voice Scheduler is Android only.
